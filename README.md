# Byondbites — Food Intelligence for Saudi & GCC

**Byondbites** is an AI-powered food and health intelligence app. Scan what’s in your fridge or pantry, get **personalized recipes**, and ask **Ayra** — your AI food companion — what you should eat.

> Built for **Saudi Arabia and the GCC** first. Arabic experience included.

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-byondbites.com-2E7D32)](https://byondbites.com)

---

## The problem

People don’t need more recipes.  
They need **better everyday food decisions** — with the ingredients they already have.

## What Byondbites does

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Scan ingredients** | Turn fridge / pantry items into meal ideas |
| **Personalized recipes** | Based on preferences, allergies, and goals |
| **Ayra** | AI companion for “what should I eat?” |
| **Health Mode** | Practical nutrition guidance (not medical advice) |
| **Arabic + English** | Built for how people actually eat in Saudi & GCC |
