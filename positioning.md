# Byondbites positioning

**Category:** Food Intelligence (not “just a recipe app”)

**One-liner:** Personalized food decisions from what you have, what you like, and what your goals need — powered by Ayra.

**Journey:** At home → planning → shopping → restaurant → health context

**Market:** Saudi Arabia first, then GCC, then global

**Brand feel:** Caregiver + Magician — warm, smart, simple, human

**Do not claim:** Medical diagnosis or treatment. Health Mode = practical food/wellness guidance.
